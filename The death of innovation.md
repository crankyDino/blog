# Diatribe of an old man

I miss the days of innovation for the sake of innovation. creating something better simply to make it better than what was already out there. to break the status quo, change how things are done merely out of bored on the weekend with fear of the outside and an inability to come up with anything original on your own so you think to yourself,

"Man, it'd be really cool this thing I like using did this other thing I really want it to do".

So off you went, with a dream and no plan no real idea of how hard it would actually be and why nobody has done it yet, but hey it's the journey that counts, right? RIGHT? 

![|600](https://media.digitalhippie.xyz/memes/realisation.png)

Alas, long gone are those days, and in their place? An AI slop factory of everything you never wanted. Your chatapp? Here's a slap some AI on that sucker. You notes app? SHEBANG, AI in there too. Why? Who knows? Guess what though...? the Autocomplete still sucks. Lets not forget AI in your washing machine cause AI definitely needs that sweet sweet data on your immaculate skid marks. A work of art, really. * chefs kiss * 

# What are we even doing here?

Genuinely? I don't know. Everything is mess. I miss coding by hand. I miss being a 2x dev who was better than the mediocre majority. I saw a post on dev daily today that prompted this. A terminal tool. I love the terminal. It feels like home. But I like it clean. No clutter. Native but ✨AEstHeTiC✨.

![|600](https://media.digitalhippie.xyz/terminal_deadspace.png)

So I couldn't believe it when I found a terminal emulator claiming to be the "Better Termux". I jumped at it thinking I'd found my new terminal emulator, only to see it was just a cesspool for AI terminal agents. I closed their homepage faster than you Mormon at a frat party. After I played around with their dark/light mode theme a little bit. That was admittedly cool. Maybe I was too quick to disregard them but I honestly don't want to play with anything more that has me reading more code than I'm writing.

# Conclusion

My point. What is my point? My point is that can we not just leave things alone that don't need to not be left alone?
